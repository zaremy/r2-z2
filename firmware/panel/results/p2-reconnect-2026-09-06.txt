P2 — the panel's own reconnect (#101, gates AC8)
================================================
2026-09-06. 8 deliberate disconnects on the real board against the real droid.

  min 2600 ms   max 3101 ms   mean 2681 ms   n=8

WHY THIS NEEDED MEASURING AT ALL. AC8 wants `waking` to show BOUNDED PROGRESS,
and the epic states plainly that the ~12 s figure in circulation is the MAC
DAEMON'S and must not calibrate this bar. It is out by more than 4x. A bar
drawn against 12 s would sit at a quarter full and then jump, every single
time -- which reads as a hang, and would have been "correct" against a
documented number.

BOUND THE BAR BY THE MAX, NOT THE MEAN. A bar that finishes early and waits
reads as broken; one that overruns reads as a hang. 3101 ms is the observed
worst case over 8 trials, so ~3.5 s is a defensible bound with a little headroom.

THE DISTRIBUTION IS SUSPICIOUSLY TIGHT and worth naming rather than admiring:
six of eight trials are 2600 or 2650 ms, one is 2601, one is 3101. Values
clustering on 50 ms boundaries with a single 500 ms outlier looks like
QUANTISATION BY THE SCAN INTERVAL, not like a smooth distribution -- the
central estimate is therefore more trustworthy than the spread is, and the
3101 ms sample is probably one missed scan window rather than a slow reconnect.
That matters for the bar: the real tail may be a multiple of the scan window
rather than anything continuous, and 8 trials cannot see a rare double miss.

Teardown was 25-50 ms throughout, and is timed separately so our own disconnect
is not folded into his reconnect and quietly inflating it.

MEASURED ON: the panel firmware itself, not a test app -- the same r2_link that
child 5 will use.

NOT MEASURED: reconnect after a LONG absence, after R2 sleeps, or when he is out
of range. Every trial here dropped a healthy link and reconnected to a droid
that was awake, present, and had just been talking to us. That is the easy case.
