Burn-in mitigation — #101 AC9, D-021
=====================================
2026-09-06. Measured on the real panel, by the panel.

AC9 requires the evidence to record WHAT MOVES, BY HOW MUCH, AND ON WHAT
PERIOD, and says explicitly that "it is dim" is not mitigation on its own.

WHAT MOVES   the entire frame, as one unit. Every element lives in a root
             container that is repositioned; drifting elements independently
             would change the layout as it moved, which looks broken rather
             than displaced.

BY HOW MUCH  +/-4 px in x and y, walking a 4-position square:
             (0,0) -> (4,0) -> (4,4) -> (0,4). So every glyph edge sees
             background within one full cycle. 4 px because the rows are 87 px
             and the type sits well inside them: it never clips, and it is
             below the threshold at which a glanced-at instrument reads as
             moving.

ON WHAT      one step per 60 s, so a 240 s cycle. The step index is derived
PERIOD       from the clock rather than counted, so it cannot accumulate error
             and is correct after any missed tick.

DIMMING      40% after 120 s with no state change, back to 80% the moment
             anything changes -- so something worth looking at is never dimmed
             the instant it appears. The prototype used 0.42 for sleep chrome.

MEASURED, not asserted
----------------------
Two frames captured BY THE BOARD, 60 s apart, at drift steps 0 and 1:

  burnin-drift-step0-2026-09-06.png   capture #1, t+15 s
  burnin-drift-step1-2026-09-06.png   capture #2, t+75 s

Aligning one onto the other over a 13x13 offset search:

  identical at zero offset?              NO -- the frame moved
  mismatching sampled pixels at (0,0):   1333
  best alignment:  B == A shifted by dx=4 dy=0, with ZERO mismatches

Zero mismatches at (4,0) is the part that matters: the content is not merely
different, it is IDENTICAL AND PURELY TRANSLATED by exactly the designed step.

STILL UNKNOWN
-------------
This panel's actual luminance-decay characteristics. The numbers above are a
mitigation designed against the risk, not one calibrated against it -- 4 px and
40% are judgements, not measurements. What is measured is that the mitigation
does what it says.

Also not covered: a full idle period. The proof above spans 60 s, which shows
the mechanism works; it does not show behaviour over the hours D-021 actually
cares about.

WHY THIS IS HERE AT ALL
-----------------------
Child 3 shipped in #134 with no mitigation whatever -- a static frame on an
always-on OLED. The epic says mitigation must be in the render "from child 3
onward, not retrofitted after 9-12 days of UI work", because a static frame
shipped for weeks is not reversible by a later patch. This was caught by
reading child 4's dependencies, not by anything failing.
