## AC9 — burn-in mitigation

Child 3 shipped **without it** (#134) and it was added in #140. Recorded here
because the gap is more instructive than the fix: nothing failed, and nothing
was going to. It was found by reading child 4's dependencies.

What moves, by how much, on what period: the whole frame as one unit, ±4 px in
x and y, one step per 60 s around a 4-position square (a 240 s cycle). Dimming
to 40% after 120 s with no state change, back to 80% the moment anything
changes.

**Measured, not asserted.** Two frames captured *by the board*, 60 s apart, at
drift steps 0 and 1, aligned over a 13×13 offset search:

```
identical at zero offset?              NO -- the frame moved
mismatching sampled pixels at (0,0):   1333
best alignment:  B == A shifted by dx=4 dy=0, with ZERO mismatches
```

Zero mismatches at (4,0) is the load-bearing part: the content is identical and
*purely translated* by exactly the designed step. Frames:
`burnin-drift-step0-2026-09-06.png`, `burnin-drift-step1-2026-09-06.png`.

## A note on AC10's own wording

AC10 says evidence is "a photograph of the real panel plus the serial log", and
that "a browser screenshot is not evidence for anything here". The frames above
are **neither**: they are the panel's own framebuffer, read back over the same
USB cable (`firmware/panel/tools/grab_shot.sh`).

That is *stronger* than a photograph for what it certifies — it is the actual
pixels, not a picture of them under room lighting — and **weaker** for what a
photograph uniquely proves: that the glass is lit and legible to a human eye at
arm's length. The operator's phone photo on 2026-09-06 covers that, and is the
reason the type was confirmed readable at 322 ppi.

Both are needed and they certify different things. AC10's wording should be
read as requiring the human check, not as excluding a framebuffer dump.

## The v5 prototype is stale on states, and its "nine" is a trap

`R2Z2-vault/.../Prototypes/README.md` calls `panel-v5-interactive.html` *"the
reference the LVGL firmware should match"*, and records that v5 settled **"nine
state entries, because `OFFLINE` splits three ways — NET, R2, LLM"**. The
prototype also uses `LISTENING`.

**D-017 Amendment B (2026-09-06) ruled otherwise and is normative.** The three
`OFFLINE_*` are display modes of one canonical `offline`; the name is `listen`;
`wake` is a state while `waking` is a panel view. The prototype's three offline
SCREENS are correct as screens and wrong as states.

**The number is the trap.** D-017's rank is nine states with **one** `offline`.
v5's nine spends three of its entries on the offline split. They are different
nines, and anyone matching "nine" to "nine" will conclude the two documents
agree when they do not — the same two-honest-documents-jointly-misleading
failure that already cost this project a design built on an LED fade the
fixtures cannot render.

Recorded here because the vault is gitignored: a warning that lives only there
does not survive a fresh clone, and the prototype is what a new reader is told
to build from. The vault README carries the same note.

The prototype remains the reference for **layout, type and interaction** — and
on type it is worth noting the shipped firmware does NOT match it: v5 specifies
Michroma for display and Share Tech Mono for values; `panel_ui.c` uses
Montserrat, because that is what the LVGL build ships. That is an unclosed gap,
not a decision.
